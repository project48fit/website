# Project Fitness Brand Guardrails

## Positioning
Project Fitness provides premium fitness and nutrition coaching for founders and executives. The service is physical performance, not business consulting. The work is strength training, conditioning, recovery habits, and nutrition guidance tailored to high responsibility schedules and travel.

## Audience
Founders, executives, and operators who value precision, privacy, and measurable progress. They want energy, clarity, resilience, and consistency they can trust.

## Outcomes to Emphasize
- Energy and stamina across long days
- Cognitive clarity and decision quality
- Stress resilience and travel proof consistency
- Strength, conditioning, and body composition improvements

## Brand Qualities
- Calm authority
- Clear and direct
- Private client feel
- Measured confidence

## Hard Rules
- No scarcity language
- No hype or cheesy promises
- No SaaS or marketing tech tone
- No em dash characters
- Avoid generic AI filler and long preambles

## Approved Language
- Private coaching
- Executive fitness coaching
- Strength training and conditioning
- Nutrition coaching
- Performance under pressure

## Banned Language Examples
- Last chance
- Limited seats
- Crush your goals
- Game changer
- Built for scale

## Allowed Surfaces
- Home page
- Apply page
- About page
- Coaching page
- Pricing page
- Email copy

## Never Change
- Pricing amounts
- Legal pages without approval
- Core brand colors
- Email sender domains
