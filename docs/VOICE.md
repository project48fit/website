# Voice and Copy Rules

## Tone
- Executive, precise, calm
- Short sentences
- Direct calls to action
- No fluff

## Structure
- Lead with the outcome
- Support with the method
- End with a clear next step

## Word Choice
Use:
- private coaching
- executive fitness coaching
- strength training
- conditioning
- nutrition coaching
- accountability
- measurable progress

Avoid:
- viral marketing phrases
- SaaS terms like pipeline, funnel, scale
- vague terms like life changing, revolutionary

## Punctuation
- Do not use em dash characters
- Keep commas and periods simple

## CTA Style
- Request Private Coaching
- Book your consult
- Start your plan

## Examples
Good:
- "Executive fitness coaching focused on strength, stamina, and travel proof consistency."
- "Request private coaching to get a plan that fits your calendar."

Bad:
- "Unlock your potential with our game changing system."
- "Limited spots, apply now for exclusive access."
