# Project Conversion QA

## Purpose
Audit key pages for conversion readiness and brand compliance. Focus on booked calls per week.

## Inputs
- Pages to review
- Recent changes or draft copy
- Conversion goals

## Outputs
- Pass or fail list by page
- Actionable fixes
- Risks that could reduce bookings

## Procedure
1) Confirm the primary CTA is clear and present on each page.
2) Verify copy is unambiguous about fitness coaching.
3) Check for friction on Apply and booking flow messaging.
4) Confirm no scarcity, hype, or SaaS tone.
5) Ensure headings and buttons are accessible with focus states.
6) Record findings mapped to the North Star metric.

## Failure Modes and Red Flags
- Missing or buried primary CTA
- Copy that sounds like business consulting
- Extra steps before booking
- Language that sounds generic or AI generated

## Examples
Good:
- "Request Private Coaching" placed above the fold
- "Strength training and nutrition coaching" mentioned in the hero

Bad:
- "Learn more" as the only CTA
- "Optimize your growth with our platform"
