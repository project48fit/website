# Project Email Templates

## Purpose
Write or edit email copy that aligns with Project Fitness voice and conversion goals.

## Inputs
- Email type and recipient
- Required CTA and links
- Constraints or brand rules

## Outputs
- Subject line
- Plain text body
- HTML body outline or copy blocks

## Procedure
1) Use a short subject line that signals the next step.
2) Open with a direct thank you or action context.
3) State the next step in one sentence.
4) Use short bullets to explain what happens next.
5) End with a clear CTA and booking link.
6) Keep language calm, precise, and fitness focused.
7) Avoid em dash characters.

## Failure Modes and Red Flags
- Scarcity or hype language
- SaaS tone or jargon
- Long paragraphs without clear next step
- Missing CTA link or unclear action

## Examples
Good:
- Subject: "Thanks for applying. Book your coaching call"
- Body: "Book your 15 to 20 minute call to review goals and training history."

Bad:
- Subject: "Last chance to join"
- Body: "Unlock your potential with our revolutionary system"
